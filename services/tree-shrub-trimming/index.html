The service

What you’re actually getting.

Overgrown shrubs and low branches sneak up on a property — they block windows and sight lines, crowd walkways, and make an otherwise-maintained yard look neglected. Regular trimming keeps the shape tight and the property looking deliberate.

Trimming can ride along with your regular lawn service or run as a stand-alone visit. Either way the clippings and branches go on the trailer, not in a pile at your curb.

What’s included

Every visit, the full job.

  • Shrub trimming & shaping
  • Hedge lines kept tight
  • Tree trimming
  • Walkways, entries & windows cleared
  • Beds cleaned up after the cut
  • All debris hauled off

Common questions

Answers before you even ask.

How often should shrubs be trimmed?

It depends on the plant and the season — most Coastal Bend hedges look their best trimmed a few times a year. If you’re on the weekly or bi-weekly route we can keep an eye on them and trim as needed.

Do you haul the branches away?

Yes. Everything we cut leaves with us on the trailer — nothing bagged and left at the curb.

How big a tree can you take on?

We handle standard residential and commercial trimming. Tell us what you’ve got — if a job genuinely needs a specialty tree crew, we’ll say so instead of winging it.

How is it priced?

Quoted per job, free and no obligation. Send a photo by text and you’ll usually have a number the same day.
