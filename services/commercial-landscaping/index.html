The service

What you’re actually getting.

Curb appeal is the first thing a prospective tenant, buyer or customer judges a property on — before they ever see the inside. LRT builds and maintains the beds, borders and plantings that make a commercial property read as well-run from the street.

The difference with LRT is what happens after the install: the same crew that put the landscape in keeps it on the maintenance schedule, so beds actually fill in and hold up instead of going shaggy by the next quarter. That’s the property appearance management side of the business — one vendor responsible for how the whole site looks, year round.

Common questions

Answers before you even ask.

Do you maintain what you install?

Yes — that’s the point. Most commercial landscaping fails because nobody maintains it after the install. LRT quotes the install and the upkeep together so the property keeps looking the way it did on day one.

What kinds of properties do you take on?

Offices, retail and shopping centers, rental and multi-unit properties, HOA common areas, municipal grounds and industrial sites across the Coastal Bend.

Are you insured?

Yes — $1,000,000 general liability with a $2,000,000 aggregate, on an active commercial policy.

How do I get a price?

Call or text with the property address, or send the form. We’ll walk the site with you and quote the work per property — free, no obligation.
